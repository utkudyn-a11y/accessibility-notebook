Short version: no ads, no tracking, and no cookies for readers. Here is everything this site keeps.

## On my server

- **Comments:** only the name you type and your comment. No email, no IP address. I read every comment before it goes live.
- **Discussion Corner votes:** only a running total for each answer. Your vote is not linked to you.

## In your browser

- **Discussion Corner:** the answer you picked, so next time you see the results instead of the question. It stays until you clear your browser data. Nothing about it is sent to me.
- **Inspirations game:** which inspirations you opened, for this tab only. It is gone when you close the tab and is never sent to me.
- **Paper sheets:** which pages you have already seen in this tab, so the sheets are thrown in only once. Gone when you close the tab.

## Visitor counts

I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit which page. It sets no cookies, keeps nothing in your browser and shows me totals only.

## Email newsletter

If you subscribe, Buttondown keeps your email address so it can send you the newsletter. You can unsubscribe from any email. See [Buttondown's privacy policy](https://buttondown.com/legal/privacy).

## The one cookie

The only cookie on this site is the one I use to sign in to my admin page. Readers never get it.
